# 🎨 OVIARA — Art & Creative Studio

### Where Imagination Becomes Art.

**OVIARA** is a premium art and creative studio website designed to showcase original artwork, creative collections, personalized art services, and the artistic work of **Kaleeswari S**.

The website combines an elegant digital art gallery with an interactive e-commerce experience, allowing visitors to explore different art styles, search for artwork, add pieces to a shopping bag, save favorites to a wishlist, and explore custom art options.

---
Live demo : https://kaleeswari1.github.io/OVIARA_Art-shop/

## 🌸 About OVIARA

OVIARA is built around a simple idea:

> **Every stroke has a story.**

The brand focuses on imagination, creativity, artistic expression, and transforming ideas into meaningful visual artwork.

Instead of looking like a traditional online store, OVIARA is designed to feel like a **premium independent art studio and digital gallery**.

Visitors can explore different forms of artwork, discover original creations, save artwork they love, and eventually purchase or request personalized artwork.

# 🖼️ The Collection

The collection section showcases different forms of artwork available through OVIARA.

The website currently supports:

### ✏️ Pencil Sketches

Traditional pencil-based artwork with a minimal and expressive visual style.

### 🎨 Oil Pastels

Colorful artwork created using oil pastel techniques.

### 💧 Watercolor Art

Soft and expressive artwork using watercolor techniques.

### 🖌️ Acrylic Paintings

Bold and vibrant artwork created using acrylic painting techniques.

### 🌀 Abstract Art

Creative artwork based on shapes, forms, colors, emotions, and imagination.
